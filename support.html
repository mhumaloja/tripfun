TripFun – Support

How to play

  1. Open TripFun on the car screen while parked and choose modes: Travel guide, Quiz, Spotting bingo.
  2. Children scan the QR code on the car screen with a phone and pick a game piece.
  3. When you drive into a new municipality, the car tells about it and asks a question; answers are given on the phones. In bingo, players spot things along the road.
  4. While driving the car screen shows only the scores; the game continues on the phones and by voice.

Frequently asked

The QR code does not open on the phone

Check that the phone is online. When parked, tap the small code on the car screen to enlarge it.

A phone dropped out of the game

Reload the page. The phone returns to the same game with the same game piece.

The car screen says "Game server not responding"

The game keeps retrying by itself. Check the car's network connection; if the message stays, let us know.

The municipality does not change

Municipal borders are recognised in Finland, Estonia, Sweden, Norway and the United Kingdom. The app needs the location permission.

No speech

Check the car's volume. Speech works in the languages the car supports; otherwise the game continues silently.
