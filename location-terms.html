TripFun – Location service terms

Updated 6 October 2026

  1. Provider. GeekEdu (business ID 2677179-4, Finland), info@geekedu.fi.
  2. Purpose. TripFun uses the car's location to recognise the municipality the car is in. On entering a municipality the app may tell about it (Travel guide) and ask about it (Quiz), and the bingo deck is chosen by country.
  3. Processing. The location comes from the car's positioning (Pleos Fused Location) and is processed only on the car device. Coordinates are neither stored nor sent. Only the names of the municipality and country are passed to the phones in the same game as game content.
  4. When. Only while a TripFun game is running, including when the app is in the background. A notification on the car screen shows background use. Tracking ends when the app is closed.
  5. Permission. Location requires the location permission given by the car's user. It can be withdrawn at any time in the car settings; the game then works without municipality features.
  6. Retention. Location data is not kept. The game state may include the name of the latest municipality, which is deleted from the device after at most 12 hours.
  7. Accuracy. Municipal borders come from open data and are simplified; a border crossing may be recognised slightly early or late. Not for navigation.
  8. More information. Privacy policy: privacy.html.
