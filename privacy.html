TripFun – Privacy policy

Updated 6 October 2026

Controller

GeekEdu (business ID 2677179-4, Finland)
Privacy contact: info@geekedu.fi

In short

  • TripFun does not collect or store anyone's name, contact details or other personal data.
  • Children pick a game piece (e.g. 🦔 Hardy Hedgehog) and never type anything.
  • The car's location is used only inside the car to recognise the municipality. It is never sent anywhere.
  • There is no database: game messages pass through the server without being stored.
  • No ads, analytics or cookies.

What is processed and why

Car app

  • Location (Pleos Fused Location): used to work out which municipality the car is in, for travel guide facts and local questions. Coordinates stay in the car and are neither stored nor sent. Location is followed in the background during a game; a notification on the car screen shows this.
  • Municipality name and game content (questions, guides, scores, bingo squares) are sent to the phones in the same game. The municipality name reveals a coarse location to the game's participants only.
  • Game state (game code, game pieces, scores and bingo progress) is kept on the car device for at most 12 hours so an interrupted game can be continued. Chosen game modes are remembered until the app is removed. Nothing is backed up.
  • Speech is produced by the car's own speech synthesis (Pleos TextToSpeech); text does not leave the car.
  • Content packages (municipal borders, guides, bingo items) are downloaded per country from GitHub Pages. The route or location is never sent to Wikipedia or anywhere else.

Children's phones (web page)

  • The phone creates a random key pair kept in browser storage (localStorage) so the player stays the same when the page is reloaded. The player ID is a hash of this key and is not linked to a person.
  • The chosen game piece, answers and bingo marks are sent to the car. Messages are authenticated so nobody else can play in another player's name.
  • Browser storage is strictly necessary for the game, so no separate consent is asked. It can be removed by clearing the site data in the browser.

Service providers

  • Supabase Inc. (Realtime): relays game messages between the car and phones. Messages are not stored; the provider may process technical data such as IP addresses in its logs.
  • GitHub Inc. (GitHub Pages): serves the web page and content packages.
  • jsDelivr: serves the Supabase library used by the web page.

These services see the device's IP address during a connection and may process data outside the EU/EEA. Transfers rely on the providers' data processing terms and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Data is never sold or shared with others.

Legal basis and retention

Processing is based on providing the game at the user's request and on the controller's legitimate interest in keeping the service working (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)). No game data is kept on the server; on the car device the game state is deleted after 12 hours.

Children

TripFun is a family game. Children are not asked for, and no personal data or free text is collected from them. A parent starts the game on the car screen and children join with the parent's permission by scanning the QR code.

Your rights

You have the right to information about the processing, to request correction or erasure and to object to processing. Because TripFun stores no personal data on a server, you can delete the data yourself: in the car by removing the app or its data, on a phone by clearing the site data. You can lodge a complaint with the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

Changes

We update this policy if the app's data processing changes. The date of the latest update is shown at the top.
